# Bonus Basket
 
A web-based application that helps users find the nearest store where they can get everything on their shopping list within their budget — while guaranteeing they save money along the way. As a reward, the app shows the user their total savings and offers them a small treat once they're done.
 
> **Status: Work in progress.** This repo contains two versions (`v1/` and `v2/`) as the project evolves. The older version is kept alongside the newer one so progress and design changes over time are visible.
 
## Features
- Enter a shopping list and a budget
- Finds the nearest store where the full list fits within budget
- Guarantees the user saves money compared to their set budget
- Shows a summary of total savings
- Offers a "treat" reward once savings are calculated
## Why I built this
Grocery shopping on a budget is something every student deals with. This app is meant to take the guesswork out of it — showing not just where to shop, but how much you're realistically saving, with a bit of a reward on top to make budgeting feel less like a chore.
